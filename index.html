About Us

Your trusted partner for industrial and construction supplies since 2002

Why Choose M.N. GARG Trading Co.?

With over two decades of experience in the industrial supply sector, we have established ourselves as a reliable source for high-quality tools, equipment, and supplies. We serve diverse industries including manufacturing, construction, and infrastructure development.

Authorized Distributor For:

  • Messer - Global leader in industrial gases and gas cutting products
  • HiKOKI - Premium power tools and equipment (formerly Hitachi)
  • Arcon - Leading manufacturer of welding and cutting equipment
  • Freemans (FMI Limited) - Largest manufacturer of measuring tapes and precision tools in India
  • Hillson - Trusted manufacturer of safety shoes, gloves, and protective equipment

Our Commitment

As general order suppliers, we work with customers across various industries - from small workshops to large-scale construction projects. We pride ourselves on:

  • High-quality products at competitive prices
  • Exceptional customer service and support
  • Fast and reliable delivery
  • Personalized solutions for unique requirements
  • Expert product knowledge and guidance

We understand that each customer has unique needs, and we strive to provide personalized service to every client. Whether you're looking for a specific product or need help identifying the right solution for your business, our experienced team is here to assist you.
